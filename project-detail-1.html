Multipurpose Zone Near Buriganga

A 3D design and project proposal for a riverside entertainment zone in Dhaka, prepared for the CE302 course.

Course
CE302, BUET
Location
Buriganga riverfront, Dhaka
Tools
SketchUp, Enscape, AutoCAD

01.Overview

This project follows the complete lifecycle of a design process, from the foundational stages of planning to a detailed 3D visualisation. The goal was to apply a structured, professional approach to project management and execution from start to finish.

The first phase was a project inception report defining the scope, goals and constraints. Next came a formal project proposal detailing the methodology, timeline and expected outcomes. The final stage brought the concept to life as an immersive 3D design.

I modelled the site in SketchUp and used Enscape for photorealistic renderings and virtual walkthroughs, planning green spaces, walkways, seating areas and recreational facilities to maximise usability and visual appeal.
