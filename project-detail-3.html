The Urban Metabolism Project

A human-centred circular economy for Dhaka's waste, presented at a poster competition at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT).

Event
Poster competition, IUT
Team
BUET Mavericks
Focus
Waste collection & recovery

01.The problem

Dhaka's linear waste system is failing. It is causing a public health crisis and overwhelming the city's landfills, such as Amin Bazar. Waste is collected and dumped instead of being treated as a resource.

02.Our approach

We proposed a shift to a human-centred, circular economy that turns waste into valuable resources: clean energy, organic compost, and dignified jobs for a more resilient urban future.

  • Establish an efficient, multi-stream waste collection system in Dhaka.
  • Build a financially viable and socially empowering waste management model.

03.How it works

  1. Step 1

    Collect

    Consumers' waste goes to neighbourhood recovery hubs.

  2. Step 2

    Sort

    Waste is separated into recyclables, treatable waste and residue.

  3. Step 3

    Recover

    Each stream is recycled, treated at a plant, or sent to a landfill with gas capture.

Thermal treatment

Bio-oil, bio-char, biogas

Biological treatment

Fertiliser, biogas

Landfill gas

Methane, CO₂
